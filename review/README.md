# Independent model review record

Two separate sessions reviewed the new proofs. Read `triage.md` for the
findings and repairs, `fix-response.md` for the trajectory/lower-bound final
verdict, and `upper-fix-response.md` for the conditional upper-bound verdict.
The latter's hypotheses are proved in the consolidated manuscript.

The prompts contain exact source snapshots at each review stage. The reports
refer to equations and sections in those snapshots, not to the final paper's
renumbered equations. `input-hashes.json` identifies the initial historical
inputs; it does not hash the final manuscript. `../MANIFEST.json` hashes the
final artifact and these review records.

`applied-changes.md` records the repairs and consolidation. The last editorial
pass incorporates the upper reviewer's suggested derivation of the compact
limiting field from the uniform approximation lemma. There was no additional
review session after that pass. Session identifiers and model information
are in `chat-links.md`. Only visible reports are retained here; raw provider
responses remain outside the companion.

These are model reviews, not human peer review, formal verification, or
upstream acceptance. The original O87 audit did not cover these extensions.
